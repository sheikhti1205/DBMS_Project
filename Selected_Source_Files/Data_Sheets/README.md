# Data Sheets

This folder centralizes the repository Excel workbooks by source.

All tracked `.xlsx` and `.xls` files from `Selected_Source_Files` are stored here under source-named folders instead of under the old `Formatted_Data`, `Govt`, or `Non-Govt` wrappers. Examples:

- BBS workbooks are under `Data_Sheets/BBS/`
- BMD workbooks are under `Data_Sheets/BMD/`
- BWDB workbooks are under `Data_Sheets/BWDB/`
- DoE workbooks are under `Data_Sheets/DoE/`
- BRRI workbooks are under `Data_Sheets/BRRI/`
- Team 7's primary workbook is `Data_Sheets/Team-7_BD_Environment_Data_Resources.xlsx`
- File-mapping workbooks are under `Data_Sheets/File_Mapping/`

The original source folders outside `Data_Sheets` remain for PDFs, images, graph folders, notes, and other non-Excel supporting materials.

\section*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Preface}

This report presents the Database Systems Lab project undertaken by Group-07, Environment Sector, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, University of Chittagong, from 2 April 2026 to 20 August 2026, submitted as A Bangladesh Environmental Data Integration Model under the broader MODELBD sectoral data-integration initiative, in which different student groups take responsibility for different national data sectors while Group-07's assignment is the environment sector. The group designs a relational database for selected Bangladesh environmental records, gathered from official and select non-government sources, by reviewing and converting source files into structured datasets. The team
first examines the collected files through preliminary ER modelling. After
mentor review, relevant sheets are examined separately to identify candidate
entities, attributes and keys before similar structures are integrated into the
final model. This report is respectfully submitted to Prof. Dr. Rudra Pratap
Deb Nath, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, University of
Chittagong, with appreciation for the course structure and guidance connecting
database design theory to a practical environmental data-management problem.

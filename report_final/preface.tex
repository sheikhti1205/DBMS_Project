\section*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Preface}

This report presents the Database Systems Lab project undertaken by Group-07, Environment Sector, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, University of Chittagong, from 2 April 2026 to 20 August 2026, submitted as A Bangladesh Environmental Data Integration Model under the broader MODELBD sectoral data-integration initiative, in which different student groups take responsibility for different national data sectors while Group-07's assignment is the environment sector. The group designs a relational database for selected Bangladesh environmental records, gathered from official and select non-government sources, by reviewing and converting source files into structured datasets, drafting an entity-relationship diagram (ERD) for each dataset, and combining these into one final modular ERD in which record entities connect through shared supporting entities such as station, time, district, year, and river. This report is respectfully submitted to Prof. Dr. Rudra Pratap Deb Nath, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, University of Chittagong, with appreciation for the course structure and guidance connecting database design theory to a practical environmental data-management problem.
